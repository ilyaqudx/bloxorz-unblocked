🎯 How to Play Bloxorz

  • Objective: Roll your 1x1x2 rectangular stone block across the floating maze and navigate it into the square exit hole. The block must stand completely vertically on end over the hole to finish the stage.
  • Controls: Use the arrow keys or W, A, S, D to roll. On touchscreens, swipe anywhere on the canvas or tap the directional D-Pad below.
  • Avoid the Abyss: If any portion of your block extends beyond the platform edges or lands on an open chasm, it plunges into the void!
  • Passcode Resumes: Every stage features an authentic 6-digit passcode. Enter any passcode in the toolbar jump box to resume your journey anytime!

⚡ Special Tiles & Switch Mechanics

  • Normal Floor (Gray): Solid and sturdy stone. Supports the block in any standing or lying orientation.
  • Fragile Tiles (Orange): Delicate surface. Your block can safely lie horizontally or vertically across them, but standing vertically on a single orange tile will cause it to shatter instantly!
  • Soft Switches (Circle 'O'): Light-pressure switches that toggle bridges when touched by the block in any orientation.
  • Hard Switches (Cross 'X'): Heavy-duty switches that only trigger when the block stands vertically on end, demanding precise approach angles.
